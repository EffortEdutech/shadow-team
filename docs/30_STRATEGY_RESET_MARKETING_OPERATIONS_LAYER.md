# Strategy Reset: Marketing Operations Layer

## Why This Reset Exists

After Sprint 19, Shadow Team has a strong internal operations spine:

- support
- product onboarding support
- AI triage and draft replies
- knowledge base
- analytics
- product connector
- department agents
- approvals
- release readiness
- human work queue
- daily brief
- reviewed-today workflow

But the original company objective was broader than support. Shadow Team must become the missing company operations layer, including product marketing and social media marketing.

Marketing was underrepresented in the early build order. This reset corrects that before we go deeper into finance/admin, compliance, or technical automation.

## Updated Objective

Shadow Team must support:

- support
- product onboarding
- product marketing
- social media marketing
- sales follow-up
- documentation
- QA
- product management
- analytics
- finance/admin reminders
- compliance review
- technical operations

## Marketing Operating Layer

The marketing layer should help the company turn product knowledge into approved growth activity.

It should manage:

- product positioning
- target audiences
- campaign ideas
- social media drafts
- content calendars
- lead follow-up drafts
- human approval before publishing
- marketing activity analytics

## Required Safety Rule

Marketing AI is draft-only until the company adds explicit publishing connectors and approval gates.

AI may draft:

- LinkedIn posts
- Facebook posts
- X posts
- Instagram captions
- TikTok short-form scripts
- email/social follow-up drafts
- launch campaign ideas
- product positioning variants

AI may not:

- publish public content
- send outbound messages
- make official claims
- invent product facts
- mention customer data publicly
- change pricing promises
- run paid ads

## Recommended Sprint Sequence

| Sprint | Focus | Output |
|---:|---|---|
| 20 | Marketing Foundation | tables, marketing profiles, channels, campaigns, content drafts |
| 21 | Social Media Draft Workflow | Product Marketing Agent and Social Media Content Agent |
| 22 | Campaign Calendar | planned content by product, channel, date, and approval status |
| 23 | Sales Follow-up Workflow | lead follow-up drafts connected to campaigns and product interest |
| 24 | Marketing Analytics | activity, channel, campaign, and approval metrics |
| 25 | Social Connector Prep | safe outbox design for future public posting connectors |

## Sprint 20 Target

Sprint 20 should not start by posting to social platforms.

Sprint 20 should build the database and admin surface for:

- marketing profiles
- channels
- campaigns
- content drafts
- approval status
- human owner
- product association

The first usable outcome:

```text
Owner can create a campaign and draft social content for a product, but nothing is published externally.
```

## Decision

From this point, product marketing and social media marketing are first-class Shadow Team modules.

They sit beside support, not after it.
