## Product Rollout Rule

No product should be connected to production channels until it has:

- product profile
- support categories
- knowledge sources
- allowed AI actions
- restricted AI actions
- escalation rules
- approval owner
- basic analytics metrics
- marketing positioning
- social media content boundaries

## Marketing Rollout Rule

No product should use AI-assisted public marketing until it has:

- target audience
- positioning statement
- approved value propositions
- restricted public claims
- social channels in scope
- content approval owner
- brand tone notes
- competitor/sensitive-claim boundaries

AI may draft social/content ideas before these are complete, but drafts must remain internal and unapproved.

## First Connector

MyExpensio is the first connector because it has clear support and onboarding needs with medium risk. It should validate the central support workflow before higher-risk products are connected.
