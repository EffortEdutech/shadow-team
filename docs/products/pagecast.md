# Product Profile - Pagecast

## Product Identity

Product Name: Pagecast

Product Slug: `pagecast`

Priority: 6

Product Status: Active product

Product Type: Publishing, page/content, campaign, and SEO support platform

Primary Users: Creators, businesses, internal team, marketers

Secondary Users: Leads, page visitors, product owners

## Product Summary

Pagecast supports page creation, campaign pages, product pages, content publishing, and SEO-related workflows. AI can help draft page copy, structure pages, support users, and identify SEO improvements. It must not publish public content or make official claims without approval.

Main workflows:

- create page
- edit content
- publish page
- capture leads
- draft SEO content
- manage campaign/product pages

## Allowed AI Actions

AI may:

- explain page creation and publishing workflow
- draft page structures
- draft SEO titles/descriptions
- suggest content improvements
- create support tickets
- identify knowledge gaps

## Restricted AI Actions

AI must not:

- publish public pages without confirmation
- make official legal, financial, medical, religious, or guaranteed performance claims
- change pricing or business commitments
- delete pages without approval
- send public campaign messages without approval

## Escalation Rules

Escalate when:

- page content includes sensitive claims
- user requests official business or pricing commitment
- user reports public page outage
- publishing has reputational risk
- AI is uncertain about factual claims

## Risk Classification

| Risk | Examples | AI Behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Low | How to create page | Draft answer |
| Medium | SEO/content draft | Draft for approval |
| High | Sensitive public claims or pricing | Escalate |
| Critical | Public incident, legal threat, data breach | Immediate escalation |
