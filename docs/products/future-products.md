# Product Profile - Future Products

## Purpose

Every future product must receive a completed product profile before it is connected to Shadow Team.

## Minimum Required Fields

Before connection, define:

- product name
- product slug
- product owner
- product status
- product type
- primary users
- main workflows
- first AI use cases
- support categories
- product-specific categories
- knowledge sources
- allowed AI actions
- restricted AI actions
- escalation rules
- risk classification
- metrics
- connector requirements
- approval contact

## Default Rules

Until a profile is completed:

- AI may not answer externally for that product
- AI may not auto-route to product-specific workflows
- AI may only create an internal ticket tagged `unknown_product`
- human review is required
